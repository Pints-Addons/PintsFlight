Pint's Flight v1.0 - how many stops are left, and how long until you land
(WoW Classic Era / Hardcore / Forever)

REQUIRES
  Nothing. Standalone.

INSTALL
  Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_classic_era_\Interface\AddOns\PintsFlight
  (or _forever_ for WoW Forever). Restart the game.

WHAT IT DOES
  Take a flight and a small bar appears near the top of the screen:
  - Where you're going ("To Orgrimmar").
  - How many stops are left on the way ("2 stops left", then "Last leg").
  - A countdown to landing, with a progress strip underneath.
  The first time you fly a route the time is an estimate, worked out from the route's length and how fast your
  earlier flights went - it's shown with a "~". When you land, the real time is remembered, so the next flight
  on that route counts down exactly. The more you fly, the better the estimates for new routes get.
  On the flight master's map, hovering a destination adds the number of stops and the expected time to its tooltip.

HOW TO USE
  Just fly.
  /pflight          unlock the bar so you can drag it; /pflight again to lock it
  /pflight reset    put the bar back in its default spot
  /pflight disembark  show or hide the Disembark button (ends a flight at the next stop - WoW Forever)
  Middle-click and drag the bar to move it any time.
