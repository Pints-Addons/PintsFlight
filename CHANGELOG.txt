Pint's Flight - Changelog

v1.1
- Disembark: a button beside the bar ends your flight at the next flight point instead of flying on (WoW Forever -
  the game only offers it there). It shows while there's a stop before your destination; /pflight disembark hides it.
  A flight cut short isn't remembered as the route's time.
- Middle-click and drag the bar to move it any time, locked or not.

v1.0
- First public release.
- While you fly: a small bar shows where you're going, how many stops are left and a countdown to landing,
  with a progress strip.
- Learns each route: the first flight on a route is an estimate (shown with a ~), after landing the real time
  is remembered, so the next flight on that route counts down exactly.
- Flight master's map: hovering a destination adds "2 stops - about 2:40" to its tooltip.
- /pflight to unlock and move the bar, /pflight reset to put it back.
- Works on Classic Era and WoW Forever.
